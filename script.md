# Ctrl + Connect · pitch script

Ctrl + Connect · WoHack 2026 · about 398 words, roughly 177 seconds at a natural pace.

## 1. Opener

Have you ever stood outside a society event… and walked home instead?

(Pause. Let it land.)

Most of us have.

## 2. Title

This is Ctrl and Connect.

Never go alone. Never miss out.

## 3. Meet Aisha

This is Aisha. Nineteen, first year Biochemistry, commutes in from Ilford, first in her family at uni, and she works weekends.

At Freshers she saw the Asian Society stall and thought, I'll go, if someone comes with me. Nobody did.

In November a scholarship closed that she never heard about.

And now, when her flatmates go for lunch, she eats at home because it's cheaper.

None of these barriers look big. That's exactly why students like Aisha slip through.

## 4. The big issue

Aisha isn't unusual.

One in three students feel very lonely, and nearly two thirds of those have thought about dropping out.

Half have never had any career guidance.

And nearly half have cut back on seeing friends because of money.

Loneliness, missed chances and cost. That's what Ctrl and Connect goes after.

## 5. Our solution

So we didn't build another app. Nobody downloads another app.

We built into the one every Queen Mary student already opens for their timetable. Three new tiles, in the same style as everything else: Socials, Opportunities and Campus Perks.

It looks like it shipped, because that's the point.

## 6. Live demo

Scan this and you can follow along on your own phone.

(Click.) Here's Aisha's journey.

Find: she opens Socials and sees the padel social, with seven people going solo. Going alone is suddenly normal.

(Click.) Match: she signs up, the list unlocks, and she taps Match on Sana. Sana taps back, and only then do their contacts appear, with a meeting point. Nothing is shared one way.

(Click.) Apply: Opportunities shows the scholarships and insight days for first years, with deadlines, and she finds an application buddy the same way.

(Click.) Save: Campus Perks shows free food nearby right now, student deals, and where the free period products are.

## 7. Three USPs

Three things make it work.

Never go alone: see who's going solo and match with them. It's mutual, so contacts only appear if you both say yes.

Never miss out: opportunities for your year, with a buddy to apply with.

Never priced out: free food, deals and free period products.

Everyone's verified with a uni email, matches stay separate for every event, and it's open to students from other London unis too.
